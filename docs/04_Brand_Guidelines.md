# Copywriting Guidelines

Use action-oriented language.

Good Examples

- Find Your Stay
- Explore Properties
- Reserve Your Stay
- Save to Wishlist
- Contact Owner

Avoid technical or robotic wording.

---

# Trust Elements

Every property should display

- Verified Owner
- Verified Property
- Last Updated
- Reviews
- Safety Information
- Deposit Policy
- Amenities
- Nearby Colleges
