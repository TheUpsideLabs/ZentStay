# ZentStay - Product Vision

## Vision

To become India's most trusted student accommodation platform by making PG and hostel discovery simple, transparent, and reliable.

---

# Mission

Help every student find a verified and comfortable place to stay near their college without dealing with fake listings, hidden charges, or unnecessary hassle.

---

# Problem Statement

Students struggle because of:

- Fake Listings
- Hidden Charges
- Poor Information
- No Verification
- No Proper Reviews
- Poor Search Experience

Owners struggle because of:

- Vacant Rooms
- Manual Booking Process
- Low Visibility

Parents struggle because of:

- Safety Concerns
- Lack of Trust
- No Verified Information
